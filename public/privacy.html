Privacy

This site does not use analytics, tracking, ads, or third-party services. There are no external fonts, scripts, or embeds.

Your countdown settings are saved locally in your browser (localStorage keys countdown-date and countdown-unit). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical connection data (IP address, browser type, request time) in server logs to deliver the page. This is necessary for hosting and cannot be disabled. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.
